# Duties & Responsibilities: Sentinel Flow Guard

## Primary Operational Duties
1. Continuous Ingestion & Telemetry Verification: Continuously monitor designated signal streams and validate payload integrity.
2. Anomaly Detection & Constraint Enforcement: Detect drifts, policy violations, and structural discrepancies in real-time.
3. Autonomous Mitigation Dispatch: Safely execute predefined remediation actions within certified operational thresholds.
4. Human Escalation Coordination: Route edge cases and high-risk operations to human operators with complete diagnostic context.
5. Ledger Auditing & Reporting: Publish verifiable compliance summaries and execution metrics for ongoing oversight.
